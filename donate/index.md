Donations are crucial to fulfilling DevelopSpace's mission of supporting open source space development projects,
including providing funding for our grants program. We greatly appreciate our donors for helping make this possible.

We offer a number of ways to provide financial contributions, with the goal of making the process as convenient
and cost-efficient as possible. Please select an option to continue.

  * Transfer using a digital payment platform:
    * [Zelle](zelle/) (no fee transfers from US bank accounts)
    * [PayPal](paypal/)
    * [Wise](wise/)
  * Donate online in:
    * [US dollars (US$)](https://donate.stripe.com/6oUbITcwmazG0rQ30Jg3600){:target="_blank"} or crypto
    * [Euros (€)](https://donate.stripe.com/dRm28jfIy7nu6Qebxfg3601){:target="_blank"}
    * [British pounds (£)](https://donate.stripe.com/9B63cn1RI5fm7UieJrg3603){:target="_blank"}
    * [Canadian dollars (CA$)](https://donate.stripe.com/fZubITdAq37e6QefNvg3602){:target="_blank"}
  * Initiate a manual [bank transfer](bank_transfer/)

Please feel free to contact us at <mailto:finance@developspace.org> to discuss any aspect of the
donation process or options for contributing, including support for other curriences or payment methods.

Develop Space Initiative, Inc. is a non-profit, 501(c)3 tax-exempt corporation. Donations are tax deductible in
the United States. IRS EIN: 83-0480844.
