DevelopSpace supports transfers from users with Wise accounts in a number of currencies.
Note that Wise does not provide us with contact information of donors,
so please [email us](mailto:finance@developspace.org) so that we can provide a donation receipt.

You can pick one of the methods below to transfer funds:

* Send funds via Wise website or app to **finance@developspace.org**

* Donate online at <https://wise.com/pay/business/developspaceinitiativeinc>

* Scan this QR code in your Wise mobile app
