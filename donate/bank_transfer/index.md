DevelopSpace has deposit accounts available in the locations listed below for manually
initiated bank transfers, which can enable no- or low-fee donations. This option can
be particularly beneficial for larger donations. [Zelle](../zelle) is
also an option for no-fee transfers from US banks when available. Online donations in
[US dollars](https://donate.stripe.com/6oUbITcwmazG0rQ30Jg3600){:target="_blank"} and
[Euros](https://donate.stripe.com/dRm28jfIy7nu6Qebxfg3601){:target="_blank"} allow
for bank tranfers with a modest fee.

Please email us at <mailto:finance@developspace.org> if you are donating by bank transfer
or check so that we can confirm the donation and send you a receipt.

Deposit account details by location:

 * [Australia](aud.html)
 * [Canada](cad.html)
 * [Europe (SEPA)](eur.html)
 * [New Zealand](nzd.html)
 * [United Kingdom](gbp.html)
 * [United States](usd.html)

Note, it is most cost efficient to perform a domestic rather than international transfer to these
accounts, however the accounts generally accept transfers from over 100 countries.
If not located in one of the above areas, we recommend an international transfer to either the
accounts in the [United States](usd.html) or [Europe](eur.html). Transfers
from other currencies will be converted into the currency of the respective account.

Donations by check can be made out to DevelopSpace Initiative, Inc. and mailed to us at:
